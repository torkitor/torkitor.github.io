About

Clemente García-Hidalgo, MD, MSc

My work connects clinical radiology with quantitative imaging, bioinformatics and biostatistics. I am especially interested in the evidence behind imaging biomarkers and medical AI.

Clinical training

Hospital General Universitario Morales Meseguer

Radiology · Murcia, Spain

I am undertaking radiology residency training at Hospital Morales Meseguer. My research interests centre on neuroimaging, particularly perfusion MRI and the characterisation of brain tumours.

Doctoral research

IDIBELL & Bellvitge

Neuroimaging · Barcelona, Spain

My doctoral work with the Translational Imaging Biomarkers group at Bellvitge examines quantitative MRI in neuro-oncology. I work with Albert Pons-Escoda and Ignacio Martínez-Zalacaín, alongside colleagues in neuroradiology and neuro-oncology.

Across this work, I am interested in how acquisition and processing choices affect measurements, and how to evaluate findings beyond the conditions in which they were developed.
